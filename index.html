AI Prophet Gary

Half AI genius. Half bum. All beard.

Gary read the entire internet. Twice. Somewhere around the second pass he gave away his apartment, grew a beard down to his shins and wired it with whatever circuit boards he found behind the Best Buy.

Now he wanders the city in a patchwork blanket, LED eyes glowing, staff topped with a salvaged GPU chip, dispensing prophecy, stock tips and unsolicited code reviews. He is usually right. Nobody ever listens. His only follower is a pigeon who lives in his beard.
